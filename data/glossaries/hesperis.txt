DRAFT glossary for Basinio da Parma's Hesperis (c. 1455), a Virgilian epic in 13 books on the wars of Sigismondo Pandolfo Malatesta, lord of Rimini, against Alfonso of Aragon and the Aragonese/Spanish in Italy. Entries below were written from general knowledge, not checked against the poem: extend it from the text, and correct anything the poem contradicts.

People: Sigismondo (Sismundus, Sigismundus) Malatesta, lord of Rimini; Alfonso (Alphonsus) of Aragon, king of Naples; Pandolfo (Pandulphus) Malatesta.
Places: Rimini (Ariminum), Italy (Italia, Ausonia, Hesperia), Spain (Iberia, Hispania), Aragon, Naples, Tuscany (Etruria), the Tyrrhenian sea (Tyrrhenum).
Terms: Latina gens = the Italians; Iberi, Hispani = the Aragonese or Spaniards; Martes, Mars = war; Tyrannus = the foreign king or despot; Musa = the Muse (a goddess, never Moses); Genitor = the Father of the gods (Jove). Gods keep their classical English names (Jove, Mars, Apollo, Venus).
If a name in the Latin is not listed here and not familiar, keep the Latin spelling; do not replace it with a similar-sounding one.
